MikrodCAP Gateway Account Deletion

Effective Date: September 2026

MikrodTech ("we", "us", or "our") provides MikrodCAP Gateway as a companion application for businesses using the MikrodCAP platform.

If you are an authorized MikrodCAP account holder and would like to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, you can submit a request using the contact information below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your MikrodCAP account, send an email to:

support@mikrodtech.co.ke

Please use the email address associated with your MikrodCAP account where possible and include the following information:

  • The name of the business or merchant account.
  • The email address or username associated with the account.
  • A clear statement that you are requesting deletion of the MikrodCAP account and associated data.

We may request additional information when necessary to verify that the deletion request was made by the authorized account holder.

What Will Be Deleted

After a valid account deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete or anonymize account information associated with the MikrodCAP account, subject to applicable legal, security, operational, and legitimate business requirements.

Depending on the service records associated with the account, information that may be deleted or anonymized can include:

  • Account identification and profile information.
  • Authentication-related account information.
  • Business or merchant information associated with the account.
  • Customer appreciation and messaging records associated with the account, where applicable.
  • M-Pesa transaction information associated with the account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Information We May Retain

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required or permitted by applicable law, fraud prevention requirements, security purposes, dispute resolution, accounting, or other legitimate operational requirements.

Where information must be retained, it will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

SMS Messages on Your Device

Deleting a MikrodCAP account does not automatically delete SMS messages that are already stored on the merchant's Android device.

SMS messages stored on the device remain under the control of the device owner and can be managed through the device's normal SMS and Android system functions.

Processing Time

We will review and process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the request and the identity or authority of the requester.

If additional information is required to verify the request, processing may take longer until the required information is provided.

Contact Us

For account deletion requests or questions regarding the deletion of your MikrodCAP account and associated data, please contact:

MikrodTech
Email: support@mikrodtech.co.ke

For more information about how MikrodCAP Gateway handles information, please see our MikrodCAP Gateway Privacy Policy .
