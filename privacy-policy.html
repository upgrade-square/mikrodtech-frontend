Supavyza Privacy Policy

Workforce Supervision and Management Platform

Effective Date: 20 November 2026
Last Updated: 20 November 2026

Supavyza is a workforce supervision and management platform developed and operated by MikrodTech. The platform helps organizations manage employees, supervisors, work assignments, attendance, site deployments and operational activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how Supavyza collects, uses, stores, processes and protects personal information when individuals and organizations use our services.

By using Supavyza, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it before processing the relevant information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how an organization uses Supavyza, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, login credentials, account roles and organization details.
  • Employee Information: Employee names, identification numbers, contact information, employment status, enrollment dates and other workforce information entered by authorized administrators.
  • Next-of-Kin Information: Contact details of designated next-of-kin where provided by an employee or entered by an authorized organization representative.
  • Deployment Information: Worksites, posts, assigned supervisors, shifts, expected reporting times and deployment history.
  • Attendance Information: Attendance records, reporting status, absences, absence explanations and attendance history.
  • Operational Information: Patrol records, employee verification records, complaints, transfer records and operational notifications.
  • Technical Information: Device information, application logs, access records, security logs and other technical information required to maintain and secure the platform.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through Supavyza is used for legitimate workforce management and operational purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing organization accounts.
  • Managing employees and supervisors.
  • Assigning employees to worksites and shifts.
  • Monitoring and recording employee attendance.
  • Recording patrols and employee verification activities.
  • Managing employee transfers and deployment changes.
  • Generating workforce reports and operational analytics.
  • Sending relevant system and operational notifications.
  • Maintaining application security and preventing unauthorized access.
  • Improving the reliability and performance of Supavyza.

3. Location Information

Supavyza supports workforce supervision through worksite, post and deployment information.

The platform does not automatically collect an employee's real-time GPS location unless a location-based feature is introduced, enabled and appropriately disclosed.

If location-based features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what location information is collected, why it is collected and how it is used.

4. How Information Is Shared

Supavyza does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be accessible to:

  • Authorized Organization Administrators: Administrators may access workforce information belonging to their organization according to their permissions.
  • Authorized Supervisors: Supervisors may access employee deployment and operational information relevant to their assigned responsibilities.
  • Service Providers: Trusted hosting, infrastructure and technical service providers may process information where necessary to operate and maintain the platform.
  • Legal Authorities: Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

5. Data Security

MikrodTech takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Supavyza.

These measures may include:

  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Role-based access permissions.
  • Secure communication between applications and servers.
  • Protection against unauthorized access.
  • Security monitoring and operational logging.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Personal information is retained for as long as necessary to provide Supavyza services, meet operational requirements, comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Organizations are responsible for ensuring that the workforce information they provide is retained and managed in accordance with applicable laws and their internal policies.

Information may be deleted, anonymized or archived when it is no longer required, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate operational requirements.

7. User Rights and Data Requests

Individuals whose personal information is processed through Supavyza may have rights under applicable data protection laws. These may include:

  • The right to request access to personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion where legally applicable.
  • The right to object to certain forms of processing.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • The right to raise complaints with the relevant data protection authority.

Requests relating to employee information should generally be directed to the relevant organization administrator. Requests concerning the operation of Supavyza may also be submitted to MikrodTech using the contact details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Supavyza is designed for organizational workforce management and is not intended for children to use as a consumer service. We do not knowingly design the platform to collect personal information directly from children.

9. Third-Party Services

Supavyza may rely on third-party infrastructure and services to support hosting, communication, security and application functionality.

Such providers may process information on our behalf. Their processing is subject to applicable agreements, security requirements and privacy obligations.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or data processing practices.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
