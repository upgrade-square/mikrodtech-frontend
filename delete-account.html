Supavyza Account Deletion

Supavyza users can use this page to request permanent deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Enter the email address registered to your Supavyza account and submit your deletion request.

What Happens After Your Request?

MikrodTech will review your request and may contact you to verify ownership of the account.

Once verified, your Supavyza account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted, subject to applicable legal and security retention requirements.

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained where legally required or necessary for legitimate security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution purposes.
