MikrodCAP Gateway Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

MikrodTech ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how MikrodCAP Gateway handles information when you use the application.

MikrodCAP Gateway is a companion application for the MikrodCAP platform. It is designed to help participating businesses process M-Pesa payment notifications and send customer appreciation messages as part of the MikrodCAP service.

Information We Collect and Process

MikrodCAP Gateway processes information required to provide its payment-processing and customer-appreciation functionality.

  • Account Information: Information associated with your MikrodCAP account, such as your login identifier, business or merchant identification, and authentication information required to securely access the service.
  • M-Pesa Transaction Information: Information contained in relevant M-Pesa payment notification SMS messages, which may include the transaction code, payment amount, transaction date and time, customer phone number, customer name where provided in the notification, and other transaction details necessary to process the payment.
  • SMS Information: Relevant incoming M-Pesa payment notification SMS messages may be accessed and processed by the application because this functionality is required for the MikrodCAP Gateway service.
  • Customer Appreciation Messages: Information required to generate and send customer appreciation SMS messages following the processing of eligible transactions.
  • Processing and Status Information: Information about payment processing and SMS delivery status, including whether a transaction or customer appreciation message was successfully processed or failed.

MikrodCAP Gateway is not intended to function as a general-purpose SMS inbox, SMS backup application, or general messaging application. SMS access is required specifically for the core MikrodCAP payment processing and customer appreciation functionality.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate authorized MikrodCAP users and businesses.
  • To detect and process relevant M-Pesa payment notification SMS messages.
  • To extract transaction information required by the MikrodCAP platform.
  • To transmit relevant transaction information to the MikrodCAP backend for processing.
  • To generate customer appreciation messages based on eligible payment transactions.
  • To send customer appreciation SMS messages to customers.
  • To report payment and SMS processing status to the MikrodCAP platform.
  • To maintain reliable operation of the MikrodCAP Gateway, including retrying transactions when temporary connectivity problems occur.
  • To provide technical support, security, and service maintenance.

Permissions and Device Features

MikrodCAP Gateway requests certain Android permissions and device capabilities because they are required for the application's core payment-processing and customer-appreciation functionality. Depending on your device and Android version, these may include:

  • Receive SMS: Used to receive M-Pesa payment notification SMS messages required for transaction processing.
  • Read SMS: Used to access relevant M-Pesa payment notification messages so that transaction information can be extracted and processed.
  • Send SMS: Used to send customer appreciation messages after eligible M-Pesa payment transactions have been processed.
  • Internet Access: Used to securely communicate with the MikrodCAP backend and transmit transaction and processing information required by the service.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain the Gateway's required background processing so that eligible M-Pesa payment notifications can be processed reliably.
  • Background and Boot Functionality: Used to restore the Gateway's required processing after the device restarts, where the user has previously configured and authenticated the application.

These permissions are used to provide the core MikrodCAP Gateway functionality. You can manage available permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling required permissions may prevent the Gateway from processing M-Pesa payment notifications or sending customer appreciation messages.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

MikrodCAP Gateway may transmit relevant transaction, account, and processing information to the MikrodCAP backend operated by MikrodTech. This communication is necessary to provide the MikrodCAP service.

Information may be accessible to the authorized business or merchant associated with the MikrodCAP account and to authorized users of that business.

We may use trusted service providers and infrastructure necessary to operate the MikrodCAP platform, including hosting, networking, authentication, and communications services.

We do not sell, rent, or trade M-Pesa transaction information, customer information, or SMS information for unrelated advertising purposes.

We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

SMS Data

MikrodCAP Gateway accesses SMS information because its core functionality depends on processing M-Pesa payment notification SMS messages.

The application processes relevant M-Pesa notifications to identify payment transactions, extract transaction details, communicate those details to the MikrodCAP backend, and support the sending of customer appreciation messages.

SMS permissions are not used to provide unrelated messaging, advertising, or SMS management functionality.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by MikrodCAP Gateway and the MikrodCAP platform.

These measures may include secure authentication, encrypted network communication, access controls, protected authentication tokens, secure server-side processing, and security monitoring.

Because the application processes information on a user's device, the security of the device, operating system, screen lock, SIM security, and user account also plays an important role in protecting information.

We continually review the application's security practices and make improvements where appropriate.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and transaction information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the MikrodCAP service, maintain transaction records, provide technical support, maintain service reliability, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Relevant transaction information processed by the Gateway may be transmitted to and retained by the MikrodCAP backend as necessary to provide the service.

SMS messages received on the merchant's device remain subject to the device's normal SMS storage and Android system controls. Uninstalling MikrodCAP Gateway does not automatically delete SMS messages that are already stored on the device.

Merchants may contact MikrodTech regarding questions or requests concerning information associated with their MikrodCAP account.

Your Rights

  • You may manage or revoke available application permissions through your Android device settings.
  • You may stop using MikrodCAP Gateway and uninstall the application from your device.
  • Authorized merchants may contact MikrodTech regarding information associated with their MikrodCAP account.
  • You may contact us if you have questions about how MikrodCAP Gateway processes information.

Third-Party Services

MikrodCAP Gateway may use third-party infrastructure and services required to provide authentication, secure network communication, hosting, and other technical functionality.

Such services may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and are expected to maintain appropriate security measures.

MikrodCAP Gateway does not use SMS information for third-party advertising services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our practices, the MikrodCAP service, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page. We encourage you to review it regularly.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how MikrodCAP Gateway handles your information, please contact us at support@mikrodtech.co.ke . We are committed to addressing your inquiries promptly and transparently.
